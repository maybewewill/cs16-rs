# Phase 5 — Mods v1 (`phase5-mods`)

Goal: Built-in WASM modding platform (`cs-mod-api`, `cs-mod-host`, `cs-mod-macros`, `cs-mod-sdk`), pre/post hookchains across `GameDll` and `EngineApi`, deterministic `wasmi` sandbox with fuel/memory budgets and manifest permissions, `.csmod` archives + VFS `mods/<id>/` overlays, client WASM HUD mods with SHA-256 verified auto-download & `_cs16rs_mods` capability negotiation, 6 reference mods (`deathmatch`, `gungame`, `zombie`, `admin_menu`, `player_stats`, `custom_hud`), and `cargo xtask mod-build`.

1. **Step 1 — Engine & VFS Seams:** `cs-proto48` dynamic usermsg registration & raw codec, `cs-engine` `_cs16rs_mods` capability gating (`send_mod_usermsg`) & `world_collision_arc()`, `cs-vfs` mod overlay mount/unmount & `<root>/mods/<id>/` discovery.
2. **Step 2 — `cs-mod-api`:** `ABI_VERSION = 1`, `HookId` / `HookPhase` / `HookAction`, `HudDrawCmd` & chunked usermsg codecs, `ModManifest` strict `mod.toml` parser & `Permissions` bitmask, pure-Rust SHA-256, pure-Rust `.csmod` ZIP archive reader/writer.
3. **Step 3 — `cs-mod-host`:** `ModRuntime` trait (JIT seam) + `WasmiRuntime` sandbox (fuel/memory/permission limits, fault isolation), transactional server + client natives, `ServerModHost` + `ModdedGame<G>`, `ClientModTransfer` + `ClientModHost`.
4. **Step 4 — `cs-mod-macros` & `cs-mod-sdk`:** zero-dep pure `proc_macro` attributes (`#[cs_mod_init]`, `#[cs_mod_hook(...)]`, `#[cs_mod_hud]`), safe `HostBridge` + `MockHostBridge`, `HudCanvas`, `MenuBuilder`, and `scaffold_mod_project`.
5. **Step 5 — Reference Mods & `xtask mod-build`:** 6 executable `.wat`/WASM reference mods under `mods/`, `cargo xtask mod-build`, and end-to-end integration tests.
6. **Step 6 — Server & Client Wiring:** `--mods-dir` on `cs-server`, `draw_mod_hud` & mod approval prompt on `cs-client` and `cs-web`.
7. **Step 7 — CI, Parity, Docs & Merge:** update `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `docs/parity/mods.toml`, `docs/parity/checklists/phase5-mods.md`, `README.md`, run full sequential CI gate, and fast-forward merge locally into `main`.
